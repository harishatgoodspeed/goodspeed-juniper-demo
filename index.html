Step 2

Scope The Build

We asked for two briefs before touching the design: one for the homepage, one for the storefront that already has to keep working.

For the homepage

goodspeed · create_brief

task: "Build Juniper Goods' new homepage around the Series A and the three new retail partners, written to convert wholesale buyers and direct customers from the same page. Keep the live product grid underneath. No stock outdoor photography — Juniper's own shoot only. Match the rebuilt palette and the new wordmark lockup."

goodspeed sent backin 41 s
Brand
A Vermont-made outdoor goods brand moving from direct-to-consumer into wholesale.
Voice
Confident, outdoors-first, no exclamation points.
Unknown
Which three retailers to name publicly is still open.

For product & checkout

goodspeed · create_brief

task: "Rebuild the product listing, product detail and checkout on the new design system without touching the Shopify backend Juniper already depends on for inventory and fulfillment. Every live SKU carries over automatically. Checkout stays the same number of steps or fewer."

Ship & Review

Every page gets checked against the brand before it goes live. Review caught one thing on the homepage — here's the fix.
